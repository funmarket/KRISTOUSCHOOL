## 2. Accepted product contract and protected boundaries

### 2.1 Product and presentation

- One school: École Primaire Privée Kristou School in Tunisia; CP through 6ème context. No school switching, multi-school registry or shared-school tenancy.
- One canonical React app for Browser and Telegram WebView; same business features and durable data. No separate Telegram frontend, router, state store or feature copies.
- Preserve current homepage/card language, navigation geometry, eight role/public homes and Owner Teachers → Classes → Registration ordering. Oversight is inspection, not impersonation or an actor-role mutation.
- Arabic/French/English are foundational. Arabic text renders correctly within the accepted stable LTR shell/navbar; do not restore old global RTL mirroring. Local text direction may be appropriate without moving controls.
- Light is default; optional Pitch Black uses black surfaces and readable light text. Preserve the exact supplied school logo and existing media/design system. Phone first; verify narrow phones, tablet and desktop, keyboard/touch/focus/contrast/safe areas and native scrolling.
- Mock data may remain for synthetic tests/reference. Production private-data paths must not silently substitute mock people/classes, show false saved/sent/released states, or expose private data publicly.
- Reuse existing views/components. Adding a real missing workflow is allowed only under its implementation packet; preservation does not mean pretending an absent backend or absent profile screen already exists.

### 2.2 Authentication and account access

- Public sign-in: chosen username/handle OR email, plus password. Direct phone-number input is rejected before provider/resolver calls with localized guidance.
- Signup: full name, password, requested role, and email or phone (or both). Phone without email requires a chosen valid username. With email, username is optional. Full name is display information, not a login identifier. Username-only credential provisioning remains unsupported.
- Preserve Unicode username normalization/uniqueness, supported Arabic/Latin names and valid numeric-underscore handles such as `123_456`. Reject phone-like usernames. Keep phone entry separate and retain existing Tunisian/international normalization.
- Phone-backed accounts authenticate through the username resolver's internal phone credential path. Do not disable Supabase phone credentials. A secondary contact is not automatically a verified login/recovery alias. Prefill login with email or username, never phone.
- Keep existing password policy and confirmation handling; re-inspect source before freezing exact policy changes. No custom public password table, synthetic contact alias, browser-side contact lookup or heuristic identity merge.
- Seven account roles only: APP_ADMIN, MANAGER, ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL, TEACHER, SCHOOL_STAFF, PARENT, STUDENT. GENERAL_USER denotes anonymous public browsing only, never an account/enum/fallback role. Requested signup role never grants permission.
- Approval is required before private access. Contact verification, app-account approval, student admission, enrollment, guardian linking and class placement are distinct transitions.
- Preserve optional explicit Telegram linking, challenge/HMAC validation, established re-entry/cache/session behavior against the agreed `09ffacf80daa4f546140053db441abb96c68ef8b` reference. Telegram lifecycle modifications and handler redeployment remain outside GOV-01/AUTH-LOGIN-02. This plan does not revive the withdrawn Telegram rewrite.
- Future private operations in Telegram require server-verified identity and the same resource policy. A restored profile is not by itself a Supabase Auth session. Define/verify each feature's transport without silently redesigning established authentication.

### 2.6 Teacher-specific Attendance and absence explanations

Accepted: Different teachers record their own independent attendance in their assigned teaching context. Teacher A's entry must not overwrite Teacher B's. Include class, subject, teacher and date/session identity; class/day alone is insufficient. Preserve the Owner's clarification that teachers' registers are separate. Whether the SAME teacher takes a second register for repeated lessons on one day remains DG-08; do not silently decide it from a proposed schema.

Statuses: Present, Absent, Late, Excused; untouched roster entries display Not recorded. No silent present/absent default. Teaching-session states (scheduled/cancelled/completed, with final vocabulary frozen in CAL-01) are separate from pupil attendance states.

One authoritative attendance entry per pupil and approved register/session identity. Attendance history and totals read these same records. Updates amend an entry with actor/reason/version history; they do not append duplicate attendance counted twice. Preserve responsible teacher and actual recording/correcting actor separately, particularly for substitutes.

Parent justification is a separate submitted explanation/document for a linked child's absence. Authorized staff accepts/rejects/requests clarification under an approved policy; a parent's submission does not change attendance to Excused by itself. Attachments remain private; reasons must not leak to class/public feeds.

Statistics require defined eligibility, register unit, date range, Late/Excused treatment and denominator. Show honest counts before a percentage if policy is unresolved. Cancelled sessions and unrecorded registers are not pupil absences. Never derive a school-day absence simply from one teacher's lesson absence. Attendance does not change Pickup RELEASED or collector authority.

### 2.7 Substitutes and academic-year rollover

Substitute assignment records identify the real teacher, authorized class/subject/session scope and effective start/end. Preserve the original responsible teacher and history. Expiry/revocation removes current access; no credential sharing, new account roles or unbounded access. Replacement, co-teaching and past-record correction rights require DG-09 decisions.

Rollover supports academic years, enrollment renewal, promotion/retention/transfer/withdrawal and new class/teacher memberships. Preview and confirm changes with audit/idempotency. Never overwrite previous-year records, copy grades into a new year as new marks, or auto-approve accounts/guardians/collectors. Decide access to historical records explicitly; immediate current-class access removal does not justify deleting history.

### 2.8 Assessments, report cards and teacher remarks

Exact scale, weights, rounding, missing/exempt treatment, terms and publication/edit windows require school policy DG-11. Never invent a national or school grading scheme.

Assigned teachers enter permitted assessments/remarks for their teaching scope. Authorized academic administration reviews/corrects with reason/audit under approved rules. Parents/students see only their permitted child's/own published results; draft marks and restricted behavioral/safeguarding notes require separate audiences. Cross-teacher editing, administrative override and historical access must be defined. Teacher remarks are distinct from parent-originated Parent Notes, attendance notes and confidential staff case notes. Do not create unrestricted clinical or disciplinary dossiers.

### 2.11 Pickup remains independently safety-critical

WAITING → PARENT_ARRIVED → RELEASED. Parent/authorized arrival actor announces; only authorized staff physically verifies the collector and confirms release. Server checks current account, family/collector, enrollment/class, school-time dismissal window and state. Release is atomic/idempotent/audited. No parent action, attendance mark, timer, AI, geofence or notification may release a child. Keep current backend-unavailable lock until isolated + staging acceptance and explicit production activation.

Early leave is a separate staff-authorized workflow. Non-account pickup people may be school-managed authorizations. School closure/cancellation may affect dismissal only through a reviewed Pickup policy, never an accidental calendar-side effect. Live board/reconnect and delivery failure need explicit safe behavior; a committed transition with delayed notification must not be falsely described as rolled back.

### Common execution loop for implementation packets

1. Inspect current owner/source/consumers/target. Freeze exact files, interfaces, permitted effects and design-gate decisions.
2. Write the smallest relevant failing test in the existing harness; observe failure for the intended missing behavior, not a missing runtime. Document any justified test-first exception.
3. Implement the narrow domain operation and necessary consumers. Use reviewed migrations/grants/RLS before protected reads/writes, transactions for invariants, and real server identity.
4. Run focused GREEN plus neighboring regression; prove denied actors/rows and retry/concurrency cases through real enforcement. A source assertion or in-memory schedule is not database authorization/concurrency proof.
5. Inspect exact diff and protected postconditions; update only the authorities authorized by that packet. Commit/push/merge/deploy require the relevant actual authorization, not a generic step copied from a template.
6. Report evidence at the exact candidate and stop. Do not manufacture counts, claim old CI for new SHA, or treat a successful save in React state as persistence.

### Mandatory schema, RPC and frontend contract-refresh gate

Every future migration or RPC/function contract change must complete this gate within its authorized vertical slice:

1. Inspect the actual owning schema, deployed/installed version and all consuming adapters. Freeze exact input/output/error shapes and compatibility expectations; do not invent a parallel RPC name or duplicate Database type owner.
2. Replay the reviewed migration in a clean isolated Supabase environment. Validate constraints, grants, RLS and exposed Data API/RPC behavior as ordinary allowed and denied principals. A policy-present flag is insufficient.
3. Generate/refresh Supabase TypeScript database types from the exact tested schema using the supported pinned toolchain. Freeze one authoritative generated-type path in the packet after source inspection. Record schema version/fingerprint and reproducible generation command; generated types do not grant access or prove RLS.
4. Update every affected typed feature adapter, request/response validator and UI consumer against those contracts. Test real RPC/API success, errors, authorization denial and persisted readback. Compilation alone cannot prove the live endpoint returns that shape.
5. Reject drift such as a frontend selecting a column absent from the installed schema, renamed RPC parameters, missing exposure/grants, stale generated types or a pending backend disguised by local success. CI/type/contract-diff checks and real integration must detect these conditions.
6. Release compatible backend dependencies before the dependent frontend. Verify installed schema/RPC fingerprint and actual deployed app version at the release gate, with rollback/forward-fix. Do not rerun installed repair SQL, silently repair migration history or deploy Telegram handlers to make an unrelated contract check pass.

F0-R2/GOV-C1 apply this to existing reconciliation where relevant; each F1/F2/CAL/ATT/GRD/COM/PUB/REC/OPS feature task applies it to its own new schema/contract. ## 12. Release and production-readiness definition

Production-ready means the approved launch scope, not every optional future idea, has matching evidence for:

1. One canonical owner for identity/roles/records with compatible migrations, generated contracts where needed, constraints and meaningful indexes.
2. Proven current-role/resource authorization for database, API, private objects and realtime paths; old-session/relationship-removal and escalation denies.
3. Durable state across reload/retry and genuine concurrent operations; truthful UI on failure, no mock fallbacks.
4. Functional and accessible accepted mobile/trilingual/theme workflows in Browser and Telegram as applicable.
5. Configured deployment origins/secrets/session/contact flows verified at exact candidate, without exposing server credentials.
6. Tested recovery/restore and rollback/forward fix; logs/alerts, retention/audit controls and operational ownership.
7. School-approved load/performance/error objectives with actual measurements and bounded queries/imports/reports.
8. Reviewed private-data handling and official school content/policies; no invented legal/document/grade rules.
9. Clear published vs draft/inactive state, safe notification delivery and calendar/attendance/grade/Pickup semantics.
10. One consistent document set, exact production/source/evidence index, open issues and named next command.

A green build, table/RPC presence, a Vercel status, an attractive calendar or a plan checkbox alone does not meet this definition. Per-slice deployment can be accepted before whole-product readiness if its bounded scope and limitations are explicit and authorized.
