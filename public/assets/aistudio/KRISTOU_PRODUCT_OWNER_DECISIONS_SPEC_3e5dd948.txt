## 1. Why this specification exists

This is the recovered and newly agreed product contract for the current KRISTOU SCHOOL application. Another agent must be able to distinguish: (a) what the Product Owner explicitly decided, (b) what older KRISTOU plans established about domain authority, (c) what GitHub/Supabase currently implement, and (d) what still needs a design gate before code. ## 2. Product Owner decisions - ACCEPTED

### 2.1 Immutable-by-default UX and login surfaces

- Keep the current sign-in and signup screens, role selector position/options, interaction design, password/credential experience, and the current visual language as the **protected baseline**.
- Keep sign-in with email + password, phone + password, or username/handle + password, and the existing signup data contract. Do not reinvent authentication or introduce new screens or a second login system.
- Keep **optional, explicit Telegram account linking** and shared Browser/Telegram identity. No automatic merge by email, phone, handle, Telegram username, display name, or photo; do not create a second Telegram product/data store.
- Keep the seven canonical authenticated roles: `APP_ADMIN`, `MANAGER`, `ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL`, `TEACHER`, `SCHOOL_STAFF`, `PARENT`, `STUDENT`. The signup role is a *request only*.
- **No `GENERAL_USER` role.** Anonymous public visitors browse school-approved public information without creating an account. Do not revive `GENERAL_USER` fallback. Legacy text or migrations containing it are history to reconcile, not a currently accepted role.
- Keep the eight existing homepages and role-based experience. Preserve Light/Pitch Black appearance, FR/EN/AR language surfaces, and the **stable LTR top navigation**; do not impose full app-shell RTL based on outdated plans.
- Keep the Owner's requested navigation order: **Teachers -> Classes -> Registration**.
- Retain `src/data/mockData.ts` as test/reference material for now; synthetic data must never silently substitute for private real records or be represented as actual school records.

### 2.2 Account eligibility and approval

- The app is for a **single private primary school**. Authenticated school accounts are only for verified school employees, parents, and students; public visitors have no account requirement.
- Signup is never role approval. A requested `APP_ADMIN`, `MANAGER`, or ordinary role cannot self-grant privileges by choosing it from the form.
- After the school approves a person, that person uses the original signup credentials; optional Telegram linking remains independent of approval and does not grant new privileges.
- The school must have an authoritative, durable, auditable approval lifecycle, implemented through Supabase server-side authorization, not client-only flags.

### 2.3 Owner and Manager governance

- The School Owner is `APP_ADMIN`, the highest application governance role.
- An existing, authorized `APP_ADMIN` **alone** may appoint other `APP_ADMIN` or `MANAGER` accounts. Neither a Manager nor an Administrative Personnel account may appoint, elevate, reinstate, or govern these privileged roles.
- `MANAGER` operational and ordinary-account review permissions are **explicitly delegated**; the role label does not automatically confer full Owner authority.
- Preserve the existing valid Owner account. The email-dependent `handle_new_user()` Owner bootstrap is a current live defect/risk to replace with an authorized, reviewable Owner appointment process **without removing the existing Owner or enabling self-appointment**.

### 2.4 Administrative Personnel - AUTOMATIC standard role authority

Every **approved** `ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL` account automatically receives the complete standard school-administration bundle. Do **not** require individual assignment to Academic Coordinator, Parent Relations, or separate responsibility groups for routine administrative privileges.

The bundle covers:

- Academic administration: create/view/edit/manage classes, students, academic years, levels, membership, academic coordination and student transfers; view school-wide Classes and student rosters.
- Teacher support: teacher-to-class assignments, class staffing coordination, learning/curriculum support, academic schedules and authorized educational resources.
- Registration/admission: review school admission applications, coordinate required documents, enrollment processing and class placement *through the respective domain authorities*.
- Parent Relations: school-family contact, inquiries, authorized school guidance, parent communications, school documents and administrative notices, subject to privacy and safeguarding rules.
- Administrative data entry: **both individual forms and Excel (`.xlsx`) / CSV (`.csv`) import** for school records, using the same canonical validation, authorization, ownership and audit path.
- Ordinary account governance: review applications, verify affiliation, **approve, reject, suspend, revoke, and reactivate** accounts in `PARENT`, `STUDENT`, `TEACHER`, `SCHOOL_STAFF`, and `ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL`.

Boundaries of that automatic authority:

- Cannot approve, appoint, suspend, revoke, reactivate, elevate, or otherwise manage `APP_ADMIN` or `MANAGER` authority.
- Cannot perform approval, suspension, revocation, or reactivation actions on **their own account**.
- May manage other Administrative Personnel accounts within the allowed five-role set, subject to the same server-verified checks.
- Academic and office authority does not confer sovereign Owner governance, broad access to unrelated protected medical/child data, credential-reset authority, or child Pickup physical-release authority.
- The administrative role remains **one role**; organizational job descriptions are not new account roles.

### 2.5 Ordinary account review decision matrix

| Account targeted | APP_ADMIN | MANAGER | ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL | TEACHER / STAFF / PARENT / STUDENT |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `APP_ADMIN` | Owner-only governance | Denied | Denied | Denied |
| `MANAGER` | Owner-only governance | Denied | Denied | Denied |
| `ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL` | Allowed | Allowed only with explicit Manager authorization | Allowed automatically, except self | Denied |
| `TEACHER` | Allowed | Allowed only with explicit Manager authorization | Allowed automatically | Denied |
| `SCHOOL_STAFF` | Allowed | Allowed only with explicit Manager authorization | Allowed automatically | Denied |
| `PARENT` | Allowed | Allowed only with explicit Manager authorization | Allowed automatically | Denied |
| `STUDENT` | Allowed | Allowed only with explicit Manager authorization | Allowed automatically | Denied |

For the five ordinary roles, **allow** means authorized ordinary account review, suspension/revocation, and reactivation with required school verification and audit; it does not mean bypassing school affiliation or resource checks. This is a *product target matrix*, not a claim that the current DB has these policies.

A request must be denied when the actor's authority is inactive, the actor is not approved, the actor is the target in the Administrative Personnel self-action case, the target currently holds any privileged role, the requested operation changes a role beyond the actor's authority, or the target's status/identity cannot be verified. Do not authorize from a caller-supplied `requested_role` or frontend `capabilities` value.

### 2.6 Classes and Students

- Keep current `Classes` page, class list -> student roster interaction, search/filters, and accepted design; wire it to authoritative Supabase data rather than replacing it.
- Owner `APP_ADMIN` may manage and inspect school-wide classes/students.
- `ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL` automatically has school-wide Classes/Students read/write/import authority upon approval.
- `MANAGER` receives school-wide Classes/Students authority only under explicit approved delegation.
- `TEACHER` sees assigned classes, class students and class features through **TeacherClassAssignment**, not by being given the general school-wide roster.
- `PARENT` sees only verified linked children via **ParentChildLink** and their permitted class information. `STUDENT` sees only own active class scope. Ordinary `SCHOOL_STAFF` does not inherit full administrative roster access.
- Preserve distinct owners: People (student identity), Enrollment (admission/enrollment), Academics (year/level/class, teacher assignment, student class membership), Family (guardian link), Access Control (who may act). Do not collapse these into a generic roster or attach all identities directly to `auth.users`.
- A student can have a school record without an app account. Student admission or spreadsheet import never automatically creates credentials, approves an app account, or links a parent by matching contact details.

### 2.7 Registration and student admission

Account signup (`AuthModal`) and school admission (`RegistrationView`) are DIFFERENT workflows. Keep the present screens. The historical student-admission lifecycle is:

`DRAFT -> SUBMITTED -> UNDER_REVIEW -> NEED_DOCUMENTS -> UNDER_REVIEW -> ACCEPTED / WAITLIST / REJECTED -> ENROLLED`.

An accepted student is not automatically a class member; Academics owns class membership, Enrollment owns enrollment eligibility and review. Exact legal/age/document requirements belong to school configuration, not agent guesswork.

### 2.8 Communication and Pickup safety

- Parent Notes: real persistence must respect linked child/teacher context, audience privacy, **11 notes per school-defined day**, **08:00-18:00 Africa/Tunis** sending window and the previously specified 08:00 daily reset. A local React-state success is not delivery.
- Student Class Questions: at most 200 characters, class-wide only, verified active membership, Teacher moderation, no student private DMs.
- Pickup: `WAITING -> PARENT_ARRIVED -> RELEASED`, with server-controlled school/class window. Parent arrival is NOT release. Only **authorized staff** may confirm physical release after verifying the actual collector. Authoritative records, idempotency, concurrency safety, audit, realtime/recovery, and notification correctness are required before enabling Pickup.
- Keep Pickup's current backend-unavailable **safety lock** until all safety acceptance gates are satisfied. Do not add Redis/worker/other infrastructure merely because old monorepo plans mentioned it.

## 5. Design decisions still explicitly NOT approved

These are NOT permission to improvise; they are decision gates for the relevant implementation task:

- **DG-01 Suspension vs revocation representation:** Existing frontend account statuses are `PENDING`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `REVOKED`; the Product Owner explicitly authorized *both* suspension and revocation and reactivation. Choose and review whether to add `SUSPENDED` as a distinct state, or keep existing status with an additional auditable deactivation reason. Prevent false/misleading status UI; preserve current appearance. Do not silently collapse the business meanings.
- **DG-02 Actual school import file contract:** column names, required data, school identifiers, duplicate matching rules, maximum batch size, and conflict/rollback semantics must be reviewed with school owners before real data uploads. The acceptance of both methods is fixed; the file schema is not.
- **DG-03 School admission details:** actual document requirements, admissible levels, academic year windows, official student identifiers and enrollment decision criteria must be supplied/validated by the school; do not invent Tunisian regulations.
- **DG-04 Account-role multiplicity and promotion semantics:** current `role_assignments` table can store several active roles. Server checks must deny ordinary-admin actions against any privileged role. Whether product policy will enforce exactly one active role needs an explicit reviewed migration and transition plan.
- **DG-05 Temporary/local/staging isolation:** decide the actual isolated database environment before applying migrations; no automatic production changes or costly environment creation.
- **DG-06 Operator recovery / support:** user-facing status display and suspension/revocation recovery should be specified without changing the protected login flow. Effective deactivation must apply to already-issued sessions and linked Telegram requests, not just newly issued tokens.
