- Approved `ADMINISTRATIVE_PERSONNEL` get **all standard school administrative permissions automatically**: Classes/Students create/edit/import, academic/teacher coordination, admissions, Parent Relations, school guidance and ordinary account lifecycle.

- Preserve distinct People, Family, Enrollment, Academics and Access Control domain ownership. Teacher only assigned classes; Parent only linked children. Pickup parent arrival != authorized staff release.
